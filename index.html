Who We Are

Twenty Years of
Quiet Excellence

Founded in 2004 by Alexandra Morrison, Travel Agency began with a single question: What if travel could be personal again?

In an industry racing toward automation and volume, we chose a different path. Every journey we design passes through human hands — researchers who've walked the streets, guides who know the rhythms of daily life, partners who treat their homeland as a legacy, not inventory.

Today, our collective spans 120 destinations across six continents. But our measure of success hasn't changed: one traveler, one journey, one transformed perspective at a time.
